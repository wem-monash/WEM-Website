Annual General Meeting

Our Annual General Meeting happens once a year in September. At this meeting, all current positions are cleared and every role becomes open for nomination. Members will be notified of the meeting time and location at least two weeks in advance via email and/or social media. If more than two people are nominated for the same position, the candidates will give a short speech at the meeting, after which eligible members will vote for their preferred candidate.

Ordinary General Meeting

Our Ordinary General Meeting (OGM) takes place in March or April. At this meeting, any remaining open positions will be filled, along with the nomination of our first- and second-year representatives. Just like the AGM, members will be notified of the time and location at least two weeks in advance via email and/or social media. If more than two people nominate for a position, candidates will give a short speech at the meeting, followed by a vote.

Eligibility

To run and/or vote for a position in the WEM Committee, you must be:

✮ An ordinary member of WEM for at least seven days prior to the General Meeting
✮ A Clayton student
✮ Undertaking at least one unit of study at a Victorian Campus of Monash University
✮ A valid member (not had your membership rights suspended)
✮ 18 years or older by the General Meeting Date
